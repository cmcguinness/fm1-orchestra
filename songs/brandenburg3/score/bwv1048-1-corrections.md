# Brandenburg Concerto No. 3, I: OMR corrections log

`bwv1048-1.musicxml` is Chris Sawer's Mutopia edition (IMSLP #57477, pages 1–45 of
the full score PDF), read by Audiveris 5.11 and then corrected by hand. Every
correction below was made by reading the printed page (200 dpi page images,
enlarged 2–6x where needed). The Kunst der Fuge MIDI (then `songs/brandenburg3.mid`, now `local/brandenburg3/brandenburg3-kunstderfuge.mid`)
was used **only** to point at bars that might be misread; no note was copied
from it.

## How it was checked

1. **Structure.** Every bar of every part must last 4 quarters (bar 0, the
   pickup, 0.5; bar 136, the last, 3.5). The OMR had 75 part-bars of the wrong
   length, almost all from dropped quarter rests.
2. **Locator diff.** Each part's (onset, pitch) list per bar was compared with
   the matching MIDI track (`fm1-orchestra/tools/compare_omr.py`). The OMR started with 231
   differing part-bars.
3. Each flagged bar was read off the page, in all ten staves of the system so
   that doubled parts could be compared, and either corrected (table below) or
   confirmed as the edition's reading (second table).
4. **Dynamics.** All 45 pages were scanned for dynamics and compared with the
   OMR; two missing marks were added (bars 27 and 91).
5. The output was round-tripped (MusicXML → tokens) to confirm it matches the
   corrected bars exactly, and `fm1ctl.py import-score` reads it.

Rebuild with `python songs/brandenburg3/build_bwv1048.py` (needs the gitignored
`songs/brandenburg3/sources/brandenburg3-omr.mxl`). The hand corrections live in
`bwv1048-1-fixes.txt`; the tables below are generated from it.

## Notes on the edition

- **Time signature.** The edition prints ¢ (alla breve), and the score keeps
  it (2/2). Bars still hold four quarters, and `import-score` writes 4/4 MIDI.
- **Violins in unison at the opening.** In bars 0–9 the three violin staves are
  identical (Violin I is not an octave higher as in the MIDI), and Viola III
  doubles Violas I/II at pitch.
- **Violoncello I, bar 15.** The task brief expected C#4 here. The page shows
  D4 C4 B3 C4 B3 A3 with no accidental, checked at 4x, and all four bass staves
  agree, so the score keeps C natural.
- **Accidentals carry through the bar** in the usual way (same octave only).
  Where the OMR had already applied that rule, it is not counted as a correction.
- **Articulation.** Slurs, staccato wedges and dots were kept as the OMR read
  them. They were corrected only where a bar was being fixed anyway or where an
  error was obvious: the bar 36 slur, the bar 136 fermatas, and the trills in
  bars 72 and 77, which Audiveris does not export. The locator MIDI's trill
  realisations appear only in bars 72 and 77, which suggests there are no
  other trills.
