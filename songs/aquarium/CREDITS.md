# Credits: Aquarium

`aquarium.mid`: Saint-Saëns, The Carnival of the Animals, 7. Aquarium,
generated by `fm1ctl.py import-score` from Nevin Williams' MuseScore
transcription of the Durand edition (IMSLP #904565 MusicXML, #904564 PDF;
public domain dedication).

To regenerate it, download both from
https://imslp.org/wiki/Le_carnaval_des_animaux_(Saint-Saëns,_Camille)
(Aquarium, "For Flute, Harmonica, 2 Pianos, 2 Violins, Viola and Cello
(Williams)") into `sources/` here (gitignored), then see the command in
`aquarium.py`.

Patches: Dave Benson's public-domain DX7 library (`fm1-orchestra/sysex/SOURCE.md`).
