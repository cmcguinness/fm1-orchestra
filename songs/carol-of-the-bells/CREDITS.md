# Credits: Carol of the Bells (Shchedryk)

Music: Mykola Leontovych, *Shchedryk* (1916), his setting for unaccompanied
choir of a Ukrainian New Year's chant. Leontovych died in 1921; the music is
public domain. (The English "Carol of the Bells" lyrics by Peter Wilhousky,
1936, are still under copyright; this performance has no words.)

Notes: Leontovych's four voices are transcribed in `build_carol.py`, checked
against the setting in Wikipedia's article "Shchedryk (song)".

Arrangement: ours (the eight-part bell scoring, octave doublings, the tolling
bell, dynamics and tempo), CC0 like the rest of this repository.
`carol-of-the-bells.mid` is generated by `build_carol.py`.

Patches: Dave Benson's public-domain DX7 library (`fm1-orchestra/sysex/SOURCE.md`).
